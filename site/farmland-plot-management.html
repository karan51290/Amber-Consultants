Farmland & Plot Management

Owning farmland or an open plot outside the city is easy - keeping an eye on it isn't, especially from another city or country. We visit, inspect, and maintain your land on a regular schedule, and step in the moment something needs attention.

Get a Free Site Assessment
Out of Sight, Never Out of Mind

Your land, checked on - not just when something goes wrong.

Vacant land left unattended invites the two things every absentee owner worries about: encroachment and neglect. We schedule regular visits to your farmland or plot, flag anything that needs your decision, and handle the routine upkeep ourselves - so the first you hear of a problem isn't a surprise call from a neighbour.

What We Take Care Of

Every visit, checked against the same list.

01

Scheduled Site Visits

  • Regular inspections on a schedule you set - monthly, quarterly, or after heavy rain.
  • Photos and a short report sent to you after every visit.
02

Boundary & Encroachment Checks

  • Boundary markers, fencing, and survey stones checked against your title documents.
  • Any encroachment attempt flagged to you immediately, with photo evidence.
03

Land Upkeep & Clearing

  • Overgrowth, weeds, and debris cleared so the land doesn't look abandoned.
  • Basic drainage and access-path maintenance to prevent erosion and waterlogging.
04

Fencing & Security

  • Compound walls, fencing, and gates repaired before a small gap becomes an entry point.
  • A local caretaker or watchman arranged where the plot needs a regular presence.
05

Documentation & Tax Compliance

  • Land tax, panchayat dues, and other local payments tracked and settled on time.
  • Patta, chitta, and other land records kept current and on file for you.
Why Owners Trust Us

Someone who actually walks the land, not just reads about it.

Land owned from a distance is the easiest asset to lose track of. A standing routine is what keeps it yours in more than name.

Encroachment Watch

Boundaries checked on every visit, not just when you happen to ask - so a dispute never gets a head start.

Photo-Verified Visits

Every inspection comes with dated photos and a short note - proof of what was checked, not just an assurance that it was.

One Call for Everything

Clearing, fencing, a caretaker, tax payments - arranged through the same point of contact you already use for the rest of your property.

Tell us where it is and we'll set up a visit schedule and send you an assessment.
